# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Nono is used by individuals and small trusted groups who self-host a personal digital workspace. They use the browser extension while reading the web, then return to review, search, tag, and edit saved material without leaving their own service.

## Product Purpose

Nono brings bookmarks, published content, personal assets, infrastructure, and GitHub stars into one self-hosted system. Bookmark capture should remain fast, with user-controlled titles, folders, and descriptions.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, practical, and trustworthy. The interface should feel compact and work-focused, with familiar controls and enough hierarchy for repeated daily use.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized typography, decorative glass effects, marketing-style layouts, excessive cards, loose spacing, and controls that force important fields below the fold. Do not turn the clipping workflow into a preview-heavy form or hide source provenance.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the primary task visible and editable without unnecessary navigation.
2. Preserve user control over AI-generated metadata.
3. Make provenance explicit through the original URL and clipping timestamp.
4. Reuse the established NoMoney visual and interaction vocabulary across Nono tools.
5. Prefer readable Markdown and standard web affordances for long-form review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operation. Preserve visible focus states, meaningful labels, comfortable touch targets where space permits, reduced-motion behavior, and layouts that remain usable at browser zoom and narrow viewport widths.
